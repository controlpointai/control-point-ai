ControlPointAI issued **CP-HR-DFD-001, Rev Dash**, on November 15, 2026. This is the original issue of the three-sheet engineering drawing for the EU AI Act AI-Assisted Hiring Demonstration.

The drawing follows one fictional applicant through an AI-assisted evaluation. In the worked case, the AI recommends rejection because the applicant’s relevant experience is described in different terms from the approved criteria. The human reviewer can examine the original evidence, challenge the recommendation, and record the reason. A designated human must separately authorize the disposition.

**What the drawing covers**

- **Sheet 1 — Process and test point evidence.** Seven nodes and six controlled transitions, from intake through record retention and reconstruction.
- **Sheet 2 — Control behavior and provenance.** What permits or blocks progression, what evidence was available to the reviewer, what they actually examined, and what the workflow allowed.
- **Sheet 3 — Applicable documents and revision status.** The approved project baselines, traceability to all 42 requirements, and the official EU AI Act source. Article callouts appear alongside the relevant controls on the drawing.

The drawing also includes a planned **Delta-T verification case**. Here the AI finding is correct, but a reassuring presentation could tempt the reviewer to dismiss it. The check will examine whether the reviewer can reach the original evidence and whether the workflow records the rationale and honors the resulting decision. Operating verification remains pending.
