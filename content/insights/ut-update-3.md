## Engineering Drawing Release

ControlPointAI has released **CP-UT-DFD-001, Revision A — Piping UT Demonstration: Node 1 to Node 2 Controlled Transition**, a three-sheet engineering drawing developed as part of our Piping Ultrasonic Testing (UT) Demonstration Project.

The drawing illustrates how AI-assisted analysis can support a controlled engineering process while preserving human technical authority, verification requirements, and traceability.

Revision A adds an applicable-document revision index and references the approved REQ-06 and REQ-07 evidence clarifications addressing source accessibility and reconstruction of the evidence available during human technical review.

### What's Included

**Sheet 1 — Process and Test Point Evidence**

Documents the controlled transition from inspection work-package initiation to inspection basis and criteria determination, including engineering requirements, supporting evidence, and human review and approval.

**Sheet 2 — Control Behavior and Provenance**

Illustrates the conditions governing progression, including release, hold, resolution, escalation, and the traceability of information and engineering decisions.

**Sheet 3 — Applicable Documents and Revision Status**

Identifies the requirements baseline and individual evidence-document revisions applicable to this drawing, including the approved REQ-06 and REQ-07 Revision A documents.

The technical control logic on Sheets 1 and 2 remains unchanged. Sheet counts, title blocks, and cross-sheet references have been updated.

### Engineering Governance in Practice

Meaningful human review requires access to the evidence supporting the decision. The retained record must also make it possible to reconstruct what evidence was available when that review occurred.

The supporting document revisions clarify these expectations within the existing supervisory review and transition-evidence controls. They introduce no additional approval gate or test point. The verification cases remain proposed; this release does not claim executed testing or operational validation.

The demonstration reinforces a fundamental ControlPointAI principle:

**AI may assist with analysis and information preparation. Human technical authority remains responsible for engineering determinations, validation, and approval.**

The drawing provides a tangible engineering example of how these responsibilities can be represented within a controlled operational process.
